Guilty If Charged

Privacy policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

Draft for the Money Makers student project (Carnegie Mellon, Persuasive Design). Not yet reviewed by a lawyer. Keep it true to the code: if the code changes what it collects or where it sends it, change this page in the same commit.

The short version

  • The extension asks you about a purchase before you check out on Amazon. You can skip it every time.
  • By default you're a guest: when you leave the trial, the court forgets the case. If you set a savings goal at the end, this browser keeps your cases so the court can hold you to it.
  • Nothing goes to Amazon or to our team. We don't sell data or show ads.
  • If the extension is set to use an AI model, your answers are sent to that model's provider to write the judge's lines (details below).

"Kept in this browser" means Chrome's extension storage on this computer. It is not encrypted and not synced to other devices. Removing the extension erases it, and Settings → Clear history erases it anytime.

AI mode

In scripted mode (no AI), nothing you type leaves this browser.

In AI mode, each step of the trial sends the item, its price and your answers so far to the AI service set in Settings, which returns the judge's next line:

  • OpenRouter passes the request to one of the hosts that run the chosen model (for example, DeepSeek V4 Flash). The extension asks OpenRouter to use only hosts that don't store your data or train on it (data_collection: deny). OpenRouter itself doesn't keep prompts unless an account opts in to logging.
  • Anthropic receives the request directly when Claude is selected.

Your email, name and Amazon account are never sent. Please don't type anything you wouldn't want a service provider to read.

Your choices

  • Skip the court, or choose "Don't ask about this item".
  • Stay a guest: skip the savings goal, and the court forgets the case.
  • Erase everything: Settings → Clear history, or remove the extension.
