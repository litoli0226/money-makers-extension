## Trial 1: a real need, approved in 3 rounds

Item: wireless earbuds, $48. Goal: Spring break, $270 of $600 saved.

Judge: Order in the court. Let's hear your case for the earbuds.
Judge: What made you want the earbuds today?
You: Mine broke last week and I take the bus every day.
Judge: A broken pair is a fair reason. Noted.
Judge: Will you use them in the next 30 days? Convince me.
You: Every day on my commute, and at the gym.
Judge: Daily use counts for a lot in this court.
Judge: Could you get by with something cheaper, or wait two weeks?
You: I tried a cheap pair before and they broke in a month.
Judge: The court has heard enough.
[Verdict: go for it] You said "I take the bus every day" and backed it up. That's a solid case. Spring break stays on track.

## Trial 2: an impulse buy, the witness is called

Item: a hoodie, $64. No goal set, $84 saved so far.

Judge: Order in the court. Let's hear your case for the hoodie.
Judge: What made you want the hoodie today?
You: saw it on tiktok and it looked really cozy
Judge: Honest. The court appreciates honesty and will hold it against you only slightly.
Judge: How many hoodies are currently living in your closet?
You: like four but this one is a different color
Judge: A bold new color. The court is listening, barely.
[This one's too close to call. The court calls an expert witness.]
Future you: Hi, it's you, one month from now. If you skip this, I've got $64 more saved. Honestly? I don't remember wanting it this much.
Judge: Thank you, witness. Defendant, anything you'd like to say to your future self?
You: fair enough lol
Judge: The court accepts your graceful surrender.
Judge: Last question. If it cost twice as much, would you still want it?
You: no
[That's all the questions. The court will now deliberate.]
[Verdict: save it] Your case sounded more like a want than a need right now. That's $64 straight into your savings.
